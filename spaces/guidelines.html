What Soulamore Is in the Workplace

Soulamore is a voluntary employee wellness companion. It gives people access to anonymous venting tools, SoulBot conversations, peer support, and optional financial wellness peers — entirely outside the HR reporting chain.

We sit alongside your existing EAP (Employee Assistance Programme), not as a replacement for it. We handle the ambient, ongoing support layer — the overwhelm before it becomes a crisis — so that clinical referrals are for the cases that truly need them.

Peer & Psychologist Conduct Standards

All peer supporters and psychologists operating in workplace programs are bound by additional standards beyond the standard Soulamore peer guidelines.

  • Dual confidentiality: Peers do not share employee disclosures with the employer or with each other.
  • No advisory role: Financial wellness peers offer emotional support only — not investment, tax, or legal advice.
  • Mandatory supervision: All peers in workplace programs receive bi-monthly supervision from a licensed psychologist.
  • Clear escalation paths: If an employee discloses immediate risk to themselves or others, the peer follows the Soulamore Crisis Protocol and does not act unilaterally.
  • No dual relationships: A peer or psychologist will not take on an employee who is a direct report or close personal contact.

Employee Rights

  • You have the right to use Soulamore without your employer knowing the specifics of your usage.
  • You may withdraw from any session at any time without explanation.
  • You can request deletion of your account data by emailing privacy@soulamore.com.
  • You will not face any workplace consequences for choosing not to participate.
  • If you believe a peer acted inappropriately, you can report it to safety@soulamore.com — independent of your employer.

Crisis & Escalation Protocol

In rare cases where an employee discloses immediate risk to life, Soulamore's crisis protocol activates. This means: SoulBot redirects to the crisis page, the peer disengages from casual support mode, and our care team is notified. The organisation is informed only that a safety protocol was triggered — never the content of the conversation.

External helplines — iCall (9152987821), AASRA (+91 9820466626), or Tele-MANAS (14416) — are provided to the employee immediately.
