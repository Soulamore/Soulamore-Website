Common questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no lock-in contracts. You can cancel your subscription at any time and retain access until the end of your billing period.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. Your journal entries, session notes, and anonymous posts are never shared with third parties. We are compliant with India's DPDP Act 2023.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, all major debit and credit cards, and net banking through our secure payment gateway.

Can I get a refund?

If you experience a technical issue or feel the platform wasn't as described, contact us at hello@soulamore.com within 7 days of purchase. We review each request individually.

Are the psychologist sessions on this platform licensed?

Yes. All psychologists listed on Soulamore hold valid RCI registration or equivalent licensure. You can view their credentials on their profile pages.
