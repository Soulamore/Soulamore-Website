Escalation Protocol

Soulamore's escalation protocol is built around tiered response. The peer support layer handles the majority of student interactions. Only when specific risk signals are detected does the protocol move to clinical and guardian levels.
1

Detection

A peer, SoulBot, or moderation system detects a signal through a confession, journal entry, chat message, or direct report. Signals include explicit statements of self-harm, suicidal ideation, or descriptions of ongoing abuse.

Response within: Immediate (automated) or 1 hour (peer-reported)

2

Counsellor Review

A trained counsellor reviews the signal to distinguish emotional distress from immediate risk. The student is contacted (where identity is known) or the crisis page is served proactively.

Response within: 2 hours

3

Psychologist Intervention

For ongoing risk or acute distress, a licensed psychologist takes over. A safety plan is drafted and a follow-up schedule is established.

Response within: 6 hours

4

Guardian / Institution Informed

When the risk is significant and ongoing, parents or guardians are informed. The institution's designated welfare officer is notified simultaneously. Content of disclosures is shared only as necessary for safety.

Notification within: 24 hours of risk confirmation

5

External Support Activated

Tele-MANAS (14416), child protection services, or emergency care is activated where the situation requires intervention beyond Soulamore's scope.

As situation demands
