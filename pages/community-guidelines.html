Community Guidelines

Creating a Safe Space Together

Soulamore is built on the belief that everyone deserves a safe, supportive space to share their mental health journey.

Our Core Principles

  • Compassion: We approach each other with kindness and understanding
  • Respect: We honor diverse experiences and perspectives
  • Safety: We prioritize emotional and psychological safety
  • Confidentiality: What's shared here, stays here

Expected Behavior

Please DO:

  • Listen actively and with an open mind
  • Use "I" statements when sharing experiences
  • Respect different perspectives
  • Offer support and encouragement
  • Maintain confidentiality
  • Use trigger warnings when needed

Please DON'T:

  • Give medical or professional advice
  • Minimize or invalidate others' experiences
  • Use discriminatory or hateful language
  • Share personal information about others
  • Promote self-harm or harmful behaviors
  • Harass, bully, or threaten anyone

Reporting Concerns

If you see something concerning, please report it to contact.soulamore@gmail.com. All reports are handled confidentially.
