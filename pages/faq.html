Frequently Asked Questions

General

What is Soulamore?

Soulamore is a comprehensive mental health and wellness platform that connects you with peer supporters, licensed professionals, support groups, and self-help tools.

Is Soulamore free?

Basic features like community forums, support groups, and self-help resources are free. Some services may have associated costs which will be shown before booking.

Is Soulamore for emergencies?

No. Soulamore is NOT for emergency or crisis situations. If you're in crisis, please call your local emergency number or visit our Get Help Now page.

Services

What's the difference between Peer Support and Professional Therapy?

Peer Supporters have lived experience and offer support based on shared experience. Psychologists are licensed professionals who provide clinical treatment.

How do I book a session?

Browse peer supporters or psychologists in our directory, view their profiles and availability, choose a time, and complete the booking.

Account

How do I create an account?

Click "Sign Up" or visit our registration page, enter your email and create a password, verify your email, and you're ready to start!

How do I reset my password?

Go to the login page, click "Forgot Password?", enter your email, and follow the reset instructions sent to your email.

Community

How do I report concerning behavior?

Use the "Report" button on any post or message, or email support@soulamore.in. All reports are handled confidentially within 24-48 hours.

Can I be anonymous?

Yes! You can use a pseudonym and avatar. For 1-on-1 sessions, supporters/therapists will know your account name for safety, but your identity is protected by confidentiality.
