IMPORTANT PRIVACY PROMISE

We prioritize your anonymity. We do NOT save your Name, Email, or Phone Number unless you explicitly provide them for login or contact. Your personal details are never linked to anonymous submissions. Here is how specific tools handle your data:

  • The Vent Box: Anonymous text is stored to help us identify community stress patterns, but never linked to your ID.
  • Confession Box: Real-time safety monitoring (Silent Guardian) is active to provide immediate support for high-risk content.
  • SoulBot AI: Chat memory is retained to enhance conversation but is never used to identify you personally.
  • Safety Filters: Automated systems scan for high-risk keywords to prevent harm and provide crisis resources.

AI & Intelligent Systems

Soulamore utilizes Large Language Models (LLMs) and automated intelligence to provide empathetic support and ensure community safety.

  • Intelligent Greetings: Our SoulBot uses AI to generate contextually relevant and empathetic responses.
  • Silent Guardian: An automated safety layer monitors real-time input to detect crisis indicators and provide immediate intervention resources.
  • Data Anonymization: Any data processed by third-party AI partners (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic) is strictly anonymized and is not used to train their models.
  • No Automated Decisions: Our AI systems do not make final clinical decisions. They are designed for support, monitoring, and guidance only.

Data Collection & Usage

We only collect what is strictly necessary to provide you with a safe space:

  • Account Info: When you sign up, we verify your email or phone number to prevent spam and ensure safety.
  • Profile Data: Any nickname, bio, role (User/Peer/Pro) or preferences you choose to share.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous metrics (e.g., "Vent Box used 50 times") to improve the platform.

We do NOT sell your personal data to advertisers.

Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to control your digital footprint on Soulamore:

  • Access: You can request a copy of your data.
  • Correction: You can update your profile details at any time.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account entirely from the Settings page. This action is permanent.
  • Anonymity: You have the right to use a pseudonym.

Cookies & Consents

We keep it calm and minimal. You have granular control over the cookies we set:

  • Essential Cookies (Always Active): Used exclusively to keep you logged in securely (Firebase Auth) and for basic security mechanisms. The site cannot function without these.
  • Functional & Preference Cookies: Used to remember choices like your Light/Dark mode preference.
  • Granular Control: When you first visit, you can choose "Accept All" to enable a fully customized experience, or select "Essential Only" to reject non-essential functional customization.
  • No Ad Tracking: We never set cookies for third-party advertising, pixel tracking, or cross-site behavioral targeting.

We employ state-of-the-art security to protect your data.
