## 🎯 **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

Soulamore needs **TWO specialized dashboards** to serve distinct market segments:

1. **Campus Dashboard** - For educational institutions (students, counselors, admins)
2. **Workplace Dashboard** - For corporate organizations (employees, HR, managers)

Both dashboards share core Soulamore features but have **unique requirements** based on their audience.

# 🏗️ **TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS**

## **Database Schema Additions:**

### **Campus Collections:**
```javascript
campuses/{campusId}
  - name
  - type (university/college/school)
  - studentCount
  - counselorCount
  - settings

students/{studentId}
  - studentId (university ID)
  - major
  - year
  - hostel
  - counselorId
  - wellnessPoints
  - achievements[]

counselors/{counselorId}
  - campusId
  - specialization
  - availability
  - maxStudents
  - certifications[]
```

### **Workplace Collections:**
```javascript
companies/{companyId}
  - name
  - industry
  - employeeCount
  - eapProvider
  - settings

employees/{employeeId}
  - employeeId (company ID)
  - department
  - managerId
  - hireDate
  - wellnessCredits
  - eapSessionsUsed

managers/{managerId}
  - companyId
  - department
  - teamSize
  - trainingCompleted
  - directReports[]
```
