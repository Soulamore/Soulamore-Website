## 4. Data Sharing

We share only with:
- Cloud infrastructure providers (DPA in place)
- Payment processors (Stripe/Razorpay)
- Email service providers (newsletter and transactional)
- Analytics tools (anonymised usage data)
- Legal authorities (valid legal order only)

We do not sell data.
