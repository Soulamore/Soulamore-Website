# Cookie Policy — That's Missing
**Contact:** privacy@thatsmissing.com

## Cookies We Use

| Cookie | Provider | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| session_id | That's Missing | Strictly Necessary | Login session | Session |
| csrf_token | That's Missing | Strictly Necessary | Security | Session |
| consent_prefs | That's Missing | Strictly Necessary | Consent record | 1 year |
| lang_pref | That's Missing | Functional | Language setting | 1 year |
| _ga | Google Analytics | Analytics | Usage statistics | 2 years |
| _ga_* | Google Analytics | Analytics | Session tracking | 2 years |
| mp_* | Mixpanel (if used) | Analytics | Feature analytics | 1 year |

Analytics cookies are only loaded after explicit user consent. Users can withdraw consent at any time via Cookie Settings in the footer.

## Managing Preferences
Click **Cookie Settings** in the footer. Or manage via browser settings. Disabling strictly necessary cookies will break login functionality.
