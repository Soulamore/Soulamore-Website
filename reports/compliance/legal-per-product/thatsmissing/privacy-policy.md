## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share with:
- Cloud infrastructure providers (DPA in place)
- Payment processors (Stripe/Razorpay — no wellness or sensitive data)
- Email service providers (transactional and marketing emails — opt-in only)
- Analytics tools (anonymised/aggregated usage data only)
- Legal authorities (valid legal order only)

| Data | Retention |

## 6. Your Rights

You have rights of access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, and consent withdrawal. Email privacy@thatsmissing.com. Response within 30 days.

US users: Right to opt out of sale (we do not sell data).
EU/UK users: Right to lodge complaint with national DPA.
Indian users: Grievance redressal at grievance@thatsmissing.com within 48 hours.

## 7. International Transfers
Primary servers in India. EU/UK user data transfers governed by Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 8. Security
