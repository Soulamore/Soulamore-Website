## 4. Profile Visibility and Control

You control who sees your data:
- **Public:** Visible to anyone on the internet
- **Members only:** Visible to logged-in BoozeD In members
- **Connections only:** Visible only to your connections
- **Private:** Profile hidden from search

Recruiter visibility is controlled separately via the "Open to Opportunities" toggle.

## 5. Data Sharing

We share with:
- Cloud infrastructure providers (DPA in place, India/EU regions)
- Email service providers (transactional and alert emails)
- Payment processors
- Analytics tools (aggregated/anonymised only)
- Other BoozeD In members (per your visibility settings)
- Legal authorities (valid legal order only)

We do not sell professional contact data or member lists to third parties.

## 7. Your Rights

Access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, consent withdrawal. Email privacy@boozedin.com. Response within 30 days.

For EU/UK users: right to complain to your national DPA.
For Indian users: grievance redressal at grievance@boozedin.com within 48 hours.
For US (California) users: right to know, delete, correct, opt out of sale (we do not sell).

## 8. Security
