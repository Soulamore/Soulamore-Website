## What Are Cookies
Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. We use them to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and understand how the platform is used.

## Cookies We Use

| Cookie Name | Provider | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| session_id | Soulamore | Strictly Necessary | Maintains your login session | Session |
| csrf_token | Soulamore | Strictly Necessary | Prevents cross-site request forgery | Session |
| consent_prefs | Soulamore | Strictly Necessary | Stores your cookie consent choices | 1 year |
| lang_pref | Soulamore | Functional | Remembers your language preference | 1 year |
| theme_pref | Soulamore | Functional | Remembers dark/light mode | 1 year |

**Note:** Soulamore does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics that process wellness content. We deliberately minimise third-party cookie usage given the sensitive nature of our platform.

## Categories

**Strictly Necessary** — Always active. Required for the platform to function. No consent needed.

**Functional** — Improve your experience. Active by default but can be disabled in Cookie Settings.

**Analytics / Marketing** — We do not use these on Soulamore.

## Managing Cookies

- Click **Cookie Settings** in the footer at any time to update your preferences
- You can also control cookies via your browser settings:
  - Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Cookies
  - Firefox: Settings → Privacy → Cookies and Site Data
  - Safari: Preferences → Privacy
- Disabling strictly necessary cookies will prevent login from functioning
