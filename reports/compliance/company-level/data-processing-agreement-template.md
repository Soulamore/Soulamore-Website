## 1. Definitions

- **Personal Data:** Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as defined under applicable data protection law including GDPR, India DPDP Act 2023, LGPD, CCPA, and equivalent laws.
- **Processing:** Any operation performed on personal data including collection, storage, use, disclosure, deletion.
- **Data Subject:** The individual whose personal data is being processed.
- **Sub-Processor:** Any third party engaged by the Processor to process personal data on behalf of the Controller.
- **Applicable Law:** All data protection and privacy laws applicable to the processing activities, including but not limited to GDPR, UK GDPR, India DPDP Act 2023, LGPD, CCPA/CPRA, PDPA Singapore, POPIA, and equivalents.

---

## 2. Scope and Purpose of Processing

The Processor shall process personal data only:
- As documented in **Schedule A** (Description of Processing Activities) attached to this Agreement
- On documented instructions from the Controller
- For no other purpose without prior written consent from the Controller

---

## 3. Processor Obligations

The Processor shall:

### 3a. Confidentiality
Ensure all personnel authorised to process personal data are bound by confidentiality obligations. Not disclose personal data to any third party except as required by law or as authorised by the Controller.

### 3b. Security
Implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. At minimum:
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Encryption at rest (AES-256 or equivalent)
- Access controls and role-based permissions
- Regular security assessments
- Incident detection and response capabilities

### 3c. Sub-Processors
- Not engage sub-processors without prior written authorisation from the Controller
- Current authorised sub-processors listed in **Schedule B**
- Provide 30 days' written notice before adding or replacing sub-processors
- Impose equivalent data protection obligations on any sub-processor via written agreement

### 3d. Data Subject Rights
Provide reasonable assistance to the Controller to fulfil data subject requests (access, correction, deletion, portability) within the timeframes specified by applicable law. Respond to Controller requests within 5 business days.

### 3e. Data Protection Impact Assessments
Assist the Controller in conducting DPIAs where required under applicable law.

### 3f. Breach Notification
Notify the Controller **within 24 hours** of becoming aware of a personal data breach, including:
- Nature of the breach
- Categories and approximate number of data subjects and records affected
- Likely consequences
- Measures taken or proposed

### 3g. Deletion or Return
Upon termination of the agreement or at Controller's request:
- Return all personal data to the Controller, or
- Securely delete all personal data and provide written certification within 30 days
- Retain only what is required by applicable law, notifying the Controller accordingly

### 3h. Audit Rights
Allow the Controller or its appointed auditor to conduct audits to verify compliance with this Agreement, with 30 days' prior written notice. The Processor may satisfy this obligation by providing current third-party audit reports (ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II).

---

## 4. Controller Obligations

The Controller shall:
- Ensure a lawful basis exists for all processing instructed
- Provide clear, complete processing instructions
- Ensure data subjects are properly notified about processing
- Not instruct the Processor to carry out processing that violates applicable law

---

## 5. International Data Transfers

Where processing involves transfer of personal data outside the country of origin:
- Both Parties shall comply with applicable cross-border transfer requirements
- EU/UK personal data transfers shall be governed by the Standard Contractual Clauses (EU SCCs, June 2021 version / UK IDTA) appended as **Schedule C**
- Indian personal data transfers shall comply with DPDP Act 2023 cross-border transfer provisions

---

## 6. Term and Termination

This Agreement is effective from the date of signing and remains in force for the duration of the service agreement between the Parties. Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice. Controller may terminate immediately for material breach.

---

## 7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated in [City], India.

---

## 8. Liability

Each Party's liability under this Agreement is subject to the limitations set out in the main service agreement between the Parties. Notwithstanding, neither Party excludes liability for breaches that result in regulatory fines imposed on the other Party due to the breaching Party's non-compliance.
