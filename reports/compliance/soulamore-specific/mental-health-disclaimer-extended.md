## Full Disclaimer Text

### What Soulamore Is

Soulamore is a **digital self-reflection and peer wellness platform**. It provides tools for personal journaling, mood tracking, psychoeducational content, and optional peer community features. It is designed to support self-awareness, personal growth, and general emotional wellbeing.

### What Soulamore Is Not

Soulamore is **not** a clinical, medical, or therapeutic service. Specifically:

- Soulamore is **not** a substitute for professional mental health care, therapy, counselling, or psychiatric treatment
- Soulamore does **not** employ licensed therapists, psychiatrists, psychologists, or medical doctors in any service delivery role
- Content on Soulamore — including journal prompts, wellness tips, mood tracking features, and any AI-generated responses — does **not** constitute medical advice, clinical diagnosis, or treatment recommendations
- Use of Soulamore does **not** create a therapist-patient, counsellor-client, doctor-patient, or any other professional relationship between the user and Hashlilly Private Limited or any of its personnel

**If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, suicidal thoughts, self-harm urges, or are in immediate danger — please stop and contact emergency services or a crisis line immediately. Do not rely on this app in a crisis.**

### Seeking Professional Help

If you are experiencing persistent mental health challenges, we strongly encourage you to consult a qualified mental health professional. You can find licensed professionals through:
- **India:** Vandrevala Foundation, iCall, your local hospital's psychiatry department
- **International:** Your GP/family doctor, national mental health helplines, or psychology finder tools in your country
