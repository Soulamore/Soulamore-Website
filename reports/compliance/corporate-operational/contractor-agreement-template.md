## 4. Independent Contractor Status

The Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or agent of the Company. The Contractor:
- Has no authority to bind the Company contractually
- Is not entitled to employee benefits (PF, ESI, gratuity, paid leave)
- Is responsible for their own equipment, tools, and workspace unless otherwise agreed
- May engage other clients, provided it does not conflict with their obligations under this Agreement

## 5. Intellectual Property Assignment

### 5a. Assignment of Work Product
All work product, deliverables, code, designs, content, inventions, developments, and other materials created by the Contractor in the course of performing the Services ("Work Product") shall be considered works made for hire to the fullest extent permitted by law.

To the extent any Work Product does not qualify as works made for hire, the Contractor **irrevocably assigns** to Hashlilly Private Limited all right, title, and interest in and to such Work Product, including all intellectual property rights (copyright, patents, trade secrets, and any other rights) throughout the world, in perpetuity, without additional compensation.

### 5b. Moral Rights
The Contractor waives, to the extent permitted by applicable law, all moral rights in the Work Product.

### 5c. Pre-Existing IP
The Contractor shall disclose in writing before incorporation any pre-existing intellectual property ("Background IP") that will be included in the Work Product. For all Background IP incorporated into the Work Product, the Contractor grants the Company a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide licence to use, modify, and distribute such Background IP as part of the Work Product.

### 5d. Open Source
The Contractor shall not incorporate any open-source software into the Work Product without prior written approval. Where approved, the Contractor shall document all open-source components and their licences in the project's software bill of materials (SBOM).

### 5e. Further Assurances
The Contractor agrees to execute any further documents and take any further steps reasonably requested by the Company to perfect, record, or enforce the IP assignment.

## 6. Confidentiality

### 6a. Definition
"Confidential Information" means all non-public information about the Company's business, products, users, technology, strategy, finances, partnerships, and operations, whether disclosed before or during this Agreement.

### 6b. Obligations
The Contractor shall:
- Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence
- Use Confidential Information only to perform the Services
- Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent
- Limit access to Confidential Information to those of the Contractor's personnel who need it to perform the Services, under equivalent confidentiality obligations

### 6c. Exceptions
Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that: (i) is publicly known through no fault of the Contractor; (ii) was known to the Contractor before disclosure; (iii) is independently developed without reference to Confidential Information; (iv) is required to be disclosed by law or court order (with prior notice to the Company where possible).

### 6d. Survival
Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for a period of **5 years**.

## 7. Data Protection

### 7a. Personal Data
In performing the Services, the Contractor may access personal data of users of Hashlilly products. The Contractor agrees to:
- Process personal data only on the Company's documented instructions
- Implement appropriate security measures to protect personal data
- Not disclose personal data to any third party
- Promptly notify the Company of any suspected data breach
- Upon termination, return or delete all personal data as instructed by the Company

### 7b. DPDP Act Compliance
The Contractor acknowledges their obligations as a data processor under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and shall comply with all applicable data protection laws.

### 7c. Sensitive Data — Soulamore
If the Contractor accesses systems containing Soulamore wellness or mental health data, they are bound by heightened confidentiality obligations. Such data must never be copied, shared, or retained beyond the duration of the task.

## 8. Non-Solicitation

During the term of this Agreement and for **12 months** after termination, the Contractor shall not:
- Solicit, recruit, or hire any employee or contractor of the Company
- Solicit any client, user, or partner of the Company for a competing service
