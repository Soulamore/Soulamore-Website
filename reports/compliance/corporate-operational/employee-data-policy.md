# Employee and Contractor Data Policy — Hashlilly Private Limited
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** [INSERT DATE]
**Owner:** Aditya / Hashlilly Private Limited

---

## Scope

This policy applies to all personal data collected and processed by Hashlilly Private Limited about its employees, interns, and contractors ("Staff") in the course of the employment or engagement relationship.

---

## 1. Data We Collect About Staff

### During Recruitment
- Name, contact details, CV/resume
- Educational qualifications and certifications
- Work history and references
- Interview notes and assessments
- Right-to-work documentation (PAN, Aadhaar, passport — as required by law)

### During Employment / Engagement
- Employment contract and offer letter
- Salary, compensation, and bank account details (for payroll)
- PAN number (for TDS deduction)
- Provident Fund and ESI details (where applicable)
- Performance reviews and feedback
- Leave records
- Attendance and timekeeping data
- Communication tool access logs (Slack, email — metadata only)
- System access logs (for security and IP protection)

### Special Categories (only where legally required)
- Medical certificates (for leave/insurance purposes)
- Disability information (for accommodation purposes — only with explicit consent)

---

## 2. Legal Bases for Processing

| Purpose | Legal Basis |
|---|---|
| Payroll and tax compliance | Legal obligation (Income Tax Act, PF Act) |
| Employment contract management | Contract |
| Performance management | Legitimate interests |
| Access control and system security | Legitimate interests |
| Reference checks (recruitment) | Consent |
| Background verification | Consent + Legitimate interests |

---

## 3. Data Minimisation

Collect only what is necessary. Specifically:
- Do not collect biometric data unless operationally required and legally compliant
- Do not monitor employee personal devices
- Do not access personal email or social media accounts of staff
- System access logs capture metadata (who accessed what system, when) — not content of work

---

## 4. Access and Confidentiality

- Staff HR data is accessible only to Aditya (and HR personnel when hired) on a need-to-know basis
- Payroll data is accessible only to the accountant/payroll processor and Aditya
- Staff data is never shared with other employees without a legitimate reason
- Third-party payroll processors must have a DPA in place

---

## 5. Retention

| Data Type | Retention |
|---|---|
| Recruitment records (unsuccessful candidates) | 6 months post-rejection |
| Employment records | Duration of employment + 7 years (legal obligation) |
| Payroll and tax records | 8 years (Income Tax Act requirement) |
| PF/ESI records | As required by EPF Act / ESI Act |
| Performance reviews | Duration of employment + 2 years |
| Access logs | 12 months |

---

## 6. Staff Rights

Staff have the right to:
- Access their own HR data held by Hashlilly
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of data no longer required (subject to legal retention obligations)
- Object to processing for purposes beyond their employment relationship

Requests to: privacy@hashlilly.in. Response within 30 days.

---

## 7. Contractor-Specific Notes

Contractor data (Antigravity and individuals) is handled under the same principles with the following differences:
- Contractors do not receive benefits data (PF, ESI, gratuity)
- GST and TDS records retained per tax law
- Contractor system access is revoked within 24 hours of engagement end
- All contractor credentials (API keys, repo access, AWS IAM) are audited quarterly and on offboarding

---

## 8. Offboarding Data Procedure

When a staff member or contractor ends their engagement:

- [ ] Revoke all system access within 24 hours of last day
- [ ] Revoke API keys, cloud console access, code repository access
- [ ] Request return or deletion of any Hashlilly Confidential Information
- [ ] Remind of ongoing confidentiality obligations (survive termination per contractor agreement)
- [ ] Archive HR file per retention schedule
- [ ] Remove from internal communication tools (Slack, email groups)
- [ ] Update vendor/contractor inventory if applicable
