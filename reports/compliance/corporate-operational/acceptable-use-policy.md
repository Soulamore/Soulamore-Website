## Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines what users may and may not do on any Hashlilly platform. It supplements the Terms of Service for each product and applies to all users, subscribers, and API consumers.

---

## 1. General Principles

Users must use Hashlilly platforms in a manner that is:
- Lawful under the laws of their jurisdiction and India
- Respectful of other users' rights and dignity
- Consistent with the intended purpose of each product
- Compliant with these guidelines and each product's Terms of Service

---

## 2. Prohibited Activities — All Products

The following are prohibited on all Hashlilly platforms:

### 2a. Illegal Activity
- Using any platform to plan, facilitate, or conceal any illegal activity
- Accessing or attempting to access accounts, data, or systems without authorisation
- Violating any applicable data protection, privacy, or intellectual property law

### 2b. Harmful Content
- Uploading, transmitting, or distributing malware, viruses, ransomware, or other malicious code
- Phishing, spoofing, or social engineering attacks on other users or Hashlilly systems
- Content that constitutes hate speech, incites violence, or discriminates based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, national origin, or any other protected characteristic

### 2c. Intellectual Property Violations
- Uploading content that infringes third-party copyrights, trademarks, or patents
- Reproducing or redistributing Hashlilly proprietary content or intelligence reports without authorisation

### 2d. Platform Abuse
- Using automated bots, scrapers, or crawlers to extract data from any platform
- Creating multiple accounts to circumvent access restrictions or bans
- Artificially inflating engagement metrics (likes, views, connection counts)
- Spamming other users with unsolicited messages or content
- Overloading platform infrastructure through excessive automated requests

### 2e. Privacy Violations
- Collecting, harvesting, or aggregating other users' personal information from any platform
- Sharing other users' personal information without their consent
- Stalking, harassing, or monitoring other users without their knowledge

### 2f. Impersonation
- Impersonating any person, company, brand, or Hashlilly employee
- Misrepresenting affiliation with any organisation
- Creating false or misleading profiles

## 3. Product-Specific Rules

### That's Missing
- Platform intelligence, reports, and data compilations are for internal business use only
- Commercial redistribution, resale, or licensing of Platform content requires a written commercial licence from Hashlilly
- API access is subject to rate limits; circumventing rate limits is prohibited
- Data extracted via API must not be used to build competing products without a commercial licence

### BoozeD In
- Professional profiles must represent genuine individuals or registered organisations
- Job listings must be for genuine, lawful opportunities
- Credential fabrication or misrepresentation of qualifications is prohibited
- Bulk contact extraction or harvesting of member contact details is prohibited
- Alcohol promotional content must comply with applicable advertising standards and must not be targeted at minors
- Recruiting or headhunting outside the platform's legitimate tools (e.g., using member data obtained from BoozeD In to contact people via other channels without consent) is prohibited

### Soulamore
- Content that glorifies, promotes, or encourages self-harm, eating disorders, or suicide is strictly prohibited
- Sharing other users' anonymous posts or private wellness content outside the platform is prohibited
- Using the platform to conduct unsanctioned research or collect user data is prohibited
- Posing as a mental health professional without verified credentials (if credential verification is introduced) is prohibited

## 4. Reporting Violations

If you observe a violation of this AUP:
- **All products:** Report via the in-app report function or email abuse@[product].com
- **Soulamore — urgent mental health crisis content:** Use the emergency report function for immediate review
- **BoozeD In — fraudulent listings or profiles:** report@boozedin.com

We aim to review reports within 48 hours. We will take action including content removal, account suspension, or referral to law enforcement where appropriate.

---

## 5. Consequences of Violation

Violations of this AUP may result in:
- Content removal
- Warning and formal notice
- Temporary account suspension
- Permanent account termination without refund
- Legal action where violations constitute a criminal offence or cause financial damage to Hashlilly
