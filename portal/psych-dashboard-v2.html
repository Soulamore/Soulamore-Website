Clinical Practice & Ethical Guidelines

Core professional, legal, and therapeutic standards for licensed psychologists practicing on Soulamore.

Tele-Health Confidentiality

Maintain strict client confidentiality in compliance with clinical tele-health standards and DPDP/HIPAA guidelines. All session notes and records are end-to-end encrypted.

Priority: Legal & Ethical
Duty of Care & Crisis Protocol

Continually assess client risk. If a client expresses active suicidal ideation or intent to harm others, initiate emergency crisis escalation and notify safety officers immediately.

Mandate: Emergency Protocol
Informed Consent & Boundaries

Ensure all clients receive clear informed consent detailing therapy boundaries, session structures, cancellation policies, and emergency protocol limits prior to treatment.

Best Practice
Evidence-Based SOAP Documentation

Deliver evidence-based interventions (CBT, DBT, Psychodynamic) and record detailed SOAP notes within 24 hours of session completion for continuity of care.

Standard of Care
Inter-Clinical Consultation

Participate in clinical case conferences, peer consultation, and supervisory reviews to ensure optimal diagnostic accuracy and therapeutic outcomes for complex cases.

Professional Growth
Burnout Mitigation & Self-Care

Protect your clinical efficacy by maintaining healthy caseload limits, scheduling mandatory rest buffers between clients, and practicing proactive self-care against burnout.
