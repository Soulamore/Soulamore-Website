Code of Conduct & Peer Listening Guidelines

Core ethical pillars for safe, empathetic, and confidential peer support on Soulamore.

The Code of Silence

Absolute anonymity is our foundation. Every word shared in member sessions is sacred—never disclose names, stories, or details outside the platform.

Priority: Critical
The Mirror Principle

Reflect, don't react. Your role is to listen with radical empathy and validate feelings without imposing unasked advice or personal bias.

Goal: Radical Empathy
The Boundary Wall

Keep all relationships strictly within Soulamore. Never share personal phone numbers, social media handles, or meet in person under any circumstances.

Prohibited Action
The Red Flag Protocol

Stay calm if a member mentions self-harm or crisis. Immediately trigger the red SOS safety referral to alert a clinical supervisor and provide emergency helplines.

Immediate Action
Peer-Only Scope Pledge

You are a soul-listener, not a licensed therapist. Avoid medical diagnoses or drug suggestions. Always defer serious mental health queries to psychologists.

Scope Reminder
The Self-Care Anchor

Your energy is precious. Take breaks between sessions, use the Reset tool if overwhelmed, and seek supervision for emotionally heavy cases.
