Peer Guidelines

Protocols for safe and empathetic support

The Code of Silence

Absolute anonymity is our foundation. Every word shared in these walls is sacred—never disclose names, stories, or details outside the platform.

Priority: Critical

The Mirror Principle

Reflect, don't react. Your role is to listen with radical empathy and validate feelings without imposing unasked advice or personal bias.

Goal: Validation

The Boundary Wall

Keep the relationship within Soulamore. Never share personal contact info, social media, or meet in person under any circumstances.

Prohibited Action

The Red Flag Protocol

Stay calm if a member mentions self-harm or crisis. Immediately trigger the safety referral via SoulBot or provide official emergency helplines.

Action Required

Peer-Only Pledge

You are a soul-listener, not a therapist. Avoid medical diagnoses or drug suggestions. Always defer serious mental health queries to psychologists.

Scope Reminder

The Self-Care Anchor

Your energy is precious. Take breaks between sessions, use the Reset tool if overwhelmed, and seek supervision for emotionally heavy cases.

Maintain Health

"By following these guidelines, you contribute to a positive environment where everyone feels valued and respected."
