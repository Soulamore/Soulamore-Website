Community Guidelines

How we maintain a safe space

Empathy & Respect

Treat every peer listener and community member with radical kindness. Harassment or disrespect is not tolerated here.

Baseline Rule

Sacred Privacy

Protect the shared space. Never disclose details from your sessions or community circles with anyone outside the platform.

Encryption Level

No Clinical Claims

Peers are here for support, not diagnosis. For professional medical advice, please consult our verified psychologists.

Resource Scope

Community Vigilance

If anything makes you feel unsafe, report it through the safety flag. We are a self-policing space built on mutual trust.

Safety First

Crisis Awareness

Soulamore is not for life-threatening emergencies. In a crisis, use the "Get Help Now" button for instant external resources.

Urgent Action

Active Growth

The journey of healing is continuous. Show up for your sessions, reflect in your journal, and grow with the community.
