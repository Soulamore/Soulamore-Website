Safe Space Standards

Community Guidelines

This space holds real feelings from real people. These guidelines exist so it stays that way.

What This Space Is Built On

🤝

Respect

Every story shared here belongs to a real person. Treat every word with the care you'd want shown to yours.

🪞

Anonymity

Don't share details that could identify yourself or others — names, schools, workplaces, or specific locations.

🌱

Support

We lift each other up. This is not a space to debate, challenge, or advise unless someone explicitly asks.

🔒

Privacy

What is shared here stays here. Do not screenshot, repost, or discuss someone else's confession outside this space.

What You Can Share

  • ✓ Feelings and experiences — stress, sadness, loneliness, confusion, anger, or joy
  • ✓ Thoughts you can't say aloud — fears, regrets, things you wish you could tell someone
  • ✓ Things you're processing — relationship struggles, academic pressure, identity questions
  • ✓ Gratitude or small wins — healing happens in all directions

What Is Not Allowed

  • ✕ Hate speech or slurs — targeting any person, community, religion, caste, or identity
  • ✕ Identifying others — naming or describing specific people in a way that exposes them
  • ✕ Explicit or graphic content — sexual content, glorified violence, or self-harm methods
  • ✕ Spam or promotional content — links, product mentions, or repeated identical posts
  • ✕ Medical advice — do not suggest diagnoses, medications, or treatment plans to others
  • ✕ Content that glorifies harm — romanticizing self-harm, suicide, or dangerous behaviours

How Moderation Works

1

All posts are reviewed before publishing

Nothing appears publicly until it has passed our moderation layer. This usually takes a few minutes.

2

Automated keyword filtering runs first

Posts containing certain phrases are flagged automatically and held for human review.

3

Human moderators make the final call

Our trained team reviews flagged content with context, not just keywords. Posts that violate guidelines are declined without explanation to avoid gaming the filter.

4

Crisis signals are escalated immediately

If a post suggests immediate risk to the writer, it is treated as a safety event and our care team follows up through our crisis protocol.

🆘

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, please do not wait for moderation. Get help now → or call iCall: 1800-599-0019 (free, 24/7).

Reporting a Concern

See something that shouldn't be here? Email our safety team at safety@soulamore.com. Include the post content if possible. We take every report seriously and respond within 24 hours. You will not be penalised for reporting in good faith.

The Pledge We Ask of You

"I will share honestly. I will receive gently. I will protect this space the way I want it to protect me."
